WHAT IS REAL

Real: the map and streets (OpenStreetMap), the routes (OSRM), your phone's location, and the live link between phones (Firebase).

Chosen by the app: the way with the fewest junctions. The ambulance is always moved forward along it, never back.

A guess: your lane, from your GPS. Correct it with the lane buttons. GPS is 5 to 10 m off in the open and worse between tall buildings.

Not yet: traffic lights, real traffic data, cars without the app.
